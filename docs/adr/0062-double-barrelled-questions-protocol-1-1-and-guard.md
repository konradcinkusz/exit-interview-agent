# 0062. Double-barrelled questions: protocol 1.1 and a guard rule

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-05
- Principle or guide served (or deviated from): `ai-evals` §6 and §9 (a finding the suite reports is closed in code, with the baseline regenerated under [ADR-0040](0040-baseline-gates-and-regeneration-rule.md)), `testing-strategy` §6 (a test that cannot fail is worse than none), brief §6 agent rules, [ADR-0022](0022-interview-agent-core.md) (the guard replaces a bad question by the protocol's wording)

## Context

The eval harness's independent rule ([ADR-0041](0041-mutation-proof-and-independent-rules.md)) found two things, recorded in [METHODOLOGY §12](../eval/METHODOLOGY.md#12-findings-what-the-harness-found-out-about-the-code-it-grades): `QuestionGuard.Check` accepted a question that asks two things, although its own contract says a model "cannot ... ask two things at once", and two of the protocol's own topic questions (management, culture) asked two things, which is 14 of the 60 questions in the corpus. A compound question invites a partial answer, so the second half is the one that is lost.

## Decision

- **The wording asks one thing each.** Management: "Tell me about how you were managed day to day." Culture: "How would you describe how people worked together there?" The dropped halves (how decisions were made; what was expected) are reachable through the existing single example probe, not through a second question in the opener.
- **`QuestionGuard` rejects a double-barrelled question** with the new reason `double_barrelled`, for every asking kind (topic, probe, clarification, redirect): exactly two question marks, or a second interrogative clause coordinated onto the first (`and|or` followed by what, how, why, who, whom, when, where, which). A rejected question is replaced by the protocol's wording as before. Three or more question marks keep `multiple_questions`. The reason is appended to the runner's reason list, so the numeric codes in existing traces do not shift.
- **The rule is lexical and written in the guard; it is not the harness's `IndependentRules`.** The two stay separate code so that one's bug is visible to the other (ADR-0041). Their agreement on the protocol's own wording is now tested both ways.
- **The protocol version is bumped, 1.0 to 1.1.** The version is a property of what interviewees are asked: it is stamped into every record (`interview.protocolVersion`, accepted by the schema as `major.minor`), the trace, the MCP instructions and resources, and the CLI banner. Aggregating a record that answered the compound question with one that answered the split one without the stamp would hide a change in the instrument. A minor bump because the structure (six topics, order, limits, record shape) is unchanged and 1.0 records remain valid; the file name `interview-protocol.v1.json` carries the major version only. Existing 1.0 records are left as they are; test fixtures and demo data that say "1.0" stay valid records of that version.
- **Regeneration.** The baseline is regenerated by the rule of ADR-0040 with its justification; the one behavioural change is `double_barrelled` 14/60 to 0/60 (mean tokens per interview also fall, as the questions are shorter). The MCP contract snapshot hashes change with the protocol text and are regenerated and reviewed.

## Consequences

- No deviation from a principle; no register row.
- The guard can still miss a double-barrelled question phrased without a coordinating interrogative ("as well as", "plus", a list); that is [OP-29](../OPEN-PROBLEMS.md#op-29-the-double-barrelled-check-is-lexical).
- Management no longer asks about decision-making in its opener; whether that costs coverage of that aspect is not measured by the mock profile (OP-5 and the real-model nightly profiles are where it would show).
